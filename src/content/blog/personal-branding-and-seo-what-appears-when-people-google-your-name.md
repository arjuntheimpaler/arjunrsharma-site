Before a first call, many prospects type your name into Google. What they find is part of your first impression. Managing it is a blend of personal branding and SEO.

## Audit your name results

Search your full name, and your name plus your profession, in a private browser window. Note the top ten results. Do they show what you want people to see? Are the results accurate and current? Is your own website ranking?

## Build assets that rank

- A personal website with your name in the title of the homepage and a clear About page.
- A complete LinkedIn profile with a custom URL.
- Profiles on relevant platforms and directories in your field.
- Guest articles, interviews and podcast appearances with your name.
- Author pages for any content you write for others.

## Support them with structure

Use Person structured data on your website with links to your official profiles. Keep your name, photo and description consistent everywhere. Link your profiles to each other.

## Handle unwanted results

If outdated or inaccurate results appear, update or remove the source if you can. Otherwise, build more strong, accurate assets so they rank higher. Respond professionally to any negative content.

A strong set of results is built gradually. Keep publishing and contributing, and your name will increasingly lead to material that supports your reputation.
