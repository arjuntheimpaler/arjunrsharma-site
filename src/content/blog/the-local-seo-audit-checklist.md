A local SEO audit shows what is holding your visibility back. Work through these areas in order, noting issues as you go, then prioritize fixes by impact and effort.

## 1. Google Business Profile

- Verified, with no duplicate or suspended listings.
- Business name matches real-world branding with no keyword stuffing.
- Correct primary and relevant secondary categories.
- Accurate address or service area, phone, website and hours.
- Services, description, attributes and booking links completed.
- Recent photos, posts and answered questions.

## 2. Reviews

- Total number, average rating and recency compared with local competitors.
- Share of reviews you have responded to.
- A working process for requesting reviews.

## 3. Website

- Service pages for each key service, with unique content.
- Location pages with real local detail, if relevant.
- Name, address and phone details consistent with your profile.
- Fast, mobile friendly, with click-to-call and clear calls to action.
- Title tags and headings that include services and locations naturally.
- Local business structured data and a map embed.

## 4. Citations and listings

- Core listings are claimed and accurate.
- Duplicates or outdated entries identified.
- Industry-specific and local directories covered.

## 5. Links and mentions

- Quality and relevance of existing local links.
- Opportunities from partners, associations and local media.

## 6. Competitors

- Which businesses appear in the map pack for your key searches.
- Their categories, review counts and strengths.

## 7. Tracking

- Search Console, analytics and call tracking are set up.
- Profile insights are reviewed.
- Rankings tracked across several points in your service area.

## Prioritize

Fix profile accuracy and verification issues first, then reviews, website gaps, citations and links. Record the baseline and re-audit every six months.
