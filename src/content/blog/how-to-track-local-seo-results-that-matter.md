Local SEO success is measured by customer actions, not only by rankings. Track a small set of metrics monthly to see what is working.

## From your Business Profile

Review the performance data for how many people viewed your profile, and how many called, requested directions, visited your website or messaged you. Watch trends month to month.

## From Search Console and analytics

Track organic impressions and clicks for local queries, and traffic to location and service pages. Set up conversion tracking for form submissions, calls and bookings.

## Calls and offline conversions

Use call tracking or ask every new enquiry how they found you. Many local conversions happen by phone or in person, and would otherwise go uncounted.

## Rankings

Use a local rank tracker that checks positions from multiple points around your service area, since rankings vary by location. Treat the results as a guide and focus on trends.

## Reviews

Track the number of new reviews, average rating and response rate.

Combine these into one monthly report with the business outcomes that matter, such as new customers and revenue from local channels.
