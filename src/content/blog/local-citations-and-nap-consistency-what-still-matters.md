A citation is any online mention of your business's name, address and phone number, often abbreviated as NAP. They appear in directories, social profiles, industry sites, chambers of commerce and more. Their main role today is to confirm that your business information is accurate and consistent.

## Why consistency matters

When search engines find the same details across several credible sources, they gain confidence about who you are and where you are. Conflicting information, such as old addresses, different phone numbers or variations of your name, creates doubt and can hurt visibility. It also confuses customers.

## How to audit your citations

- List your exact, current business name, address, phone number, website and hours. Use this as your master record.
- Search for your business name, old addresses and phone numbers to find existing listings.
- Check the major platforms first, including Google, Bing, Apple, Facebook, LinkedIn and relevant industry or local directories.
- Note every mismatch and every duplicate listing.

## How to fix problems

Claim listings where you can and correct the details. For duplicates, follow each platform's process to merge or remove them. Keep a log of where you are listed, with login details stored securely.

## Focus on quality

A moderate number of accurate listings on respected platforms is better than hundreds of low-quality directory submissions. Prioritize platforms your customers use, industry-specific directories and local organizations.

## Maintain it

Update every listing whenever your details change, such as moving premises or changing a phone number. Recheck the main ones twice a year.
