Local keyword research follows the same principles as general keyword research, with an added layer of place and intent. The goal is to find the phrases customers use when they need a provider nearby.

## Start with service plus place

Combine your services with the places you serve: 'physiotherapist Bandra', 'CA firm Pune', 'wedding photographer Jaipur'. Try variations with 'near me', neighborhoods, landmarks and nearby cities.

## Collect real phrases

- Use Google autocomplete and 'People also ask' for your service and location.
- Check Search Console for queries that already bring impressions.
- Review competitors' page titles and Google Business Profile services.
- Ask customers how they searched for you.

## Consider intent

Searches such as 'emergency plumber', 'open now' and 'same-day' signal urgency. Searches such as 'best' and 'reviews' signal comparison. Match your pages and profile content to these intents.

## Note the local pack

Search each phrase and see whether a map pack appears. If it does, your profile matters most. If the results are mainly organic, your website pages matter more.

Map the best phrases to specific pages and profile fields, then track the same set of phrases in different areas, since rankings vary by location.
