Plenty of professionals know their subject. Fewer have a clear point of view on it. A point of view is a consistent perspective on how things should be done and why, and it is one of the strongest differentiators in a crowded market.

## Find it in your frustrations

Think about what annoys you in your industry: advice you consider wrong, practices that waste clients' money, myths that persist. Each frustration hides a belief about a better way.

## Find it in your results

Look at the work that delivered the best outcomes. What did you do differently from the standard approach? The difference is often your point of view in action.

## State it plainly

Write it as a sentence: 'I believe that [common practice] fails because [reason], and that [alternative] works better.' Support it with evidence from your work.

## Stay consistent, and stay open

Return to your point of view across content, proposals and conversations so people associate you with it. But update it when evidence changes. Being willing to revise a view builds trust.

A well-defined perspective makes your content more distinctive, your sales conversations easier and your brand more memorable.
