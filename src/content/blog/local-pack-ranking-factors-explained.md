The local pack is the map and three listings that appear for many local searches. Appearing there brings calls, direction requests and visits. Google states that results depend on relevance, distance and prominence. Industry studies add detail on which elements tend to correlate with stronger rankings. Here is how to think about it.

## Relevance: matching the search

- Primary and secondary categories.
- Business name, where it genuinely reflects what you do.
- Services, products and description.
- Website content on the topics and services searched for.

## Distance: where the searcher is

Proximity to the searcher or to the location named in the query is a strong factor you cannot change. A business closer to the center of a city often has an edge. This is why you may rank well in your own neighborhood but not across town, and why rankings differ by exact location.

## Prominence: how well known you are

- Review quantity, average rating, recency and the content of reviews.
- Links from local and relevant websites.
- Citations and mentions across the web.
- Overall brand awareness and search interest in your name.
- Organic website strength, since strong organic rankings tend to help local visibility.

## Engagement signals

Behavior such as clicks, calls and direction requests may reflect how useful a listing is, though the exact weight is not publicly known.

## What this means for your effort

You cannot change where customers are, but you can raise relevance and prominence. Prioritize accurate categories, a complete profile, a steady stream of quality reviews, a strong website with service and location pages and relevant local links. Avoid tactics that promise quick tricks, as unnatural activity can cause suspension.
