'People also ask' is the expandable question list that appears on many search results. Each question reveals an answer pulled from a web page, often followed by more related questions. It is both a research tool and an opportunity.

## Use it for research

Search your main topic and expand the questions that appear. Each one shows what people want to know, in their own words. Keep expanding to reveal more. Collect the questions and group them by theme. This gives you a ready-made outline that reflects real searches.

## Answer the questions directly

Add the questions to the relevant page as headings, and answer each in a short, direct paragraph. Questions that are closely related can be grouped in a FAQ section on the page. Keep every answer clear enough to stand alone.

## Cover the follow-ups

The boxes often follow a logical path from basic to specific questions. Mirror that path in your content so the page covers the whole journey, from 'what is it' through 'how much does it cost' to 'how do I choose a provider'.

## Build topical depth

A single page rarely owns every question. Create supporting articles for the larger sub-questions and link them together. Search engines tend to favor sites that cover a topic thoroughly.

Revisit the boxes quarterly. The questions shift as search behavior changes, and each new question is a content idea backed by real demand.
