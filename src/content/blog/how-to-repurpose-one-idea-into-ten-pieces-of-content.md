Creating from scratch every time is exhausting. Repurposing means finding the core idea once and expressing it in several formats, each suited to a different channel and audience.

## Start with a strong source

Choose a substantial piece, such as an article, a client case study, a talk or a recorded conversation. Substance gives you material to extract.

## One idea, many forms

- A long-form article on your website.
- Three or four LinkedIn posts, each covering one point.
- A newsletter issue summarizing the lesson.
- A carousel or checklist graphic.
- A short video or audio clip of the main point.
- An answer for a community or forum question.
- A slide in your next talk or webinar.
- A section of a lead magnet.

## Adapt, do not copy

Tailor tone, length and angle to each channel. Link back to the full piece where appropriate.

Build repurposing into your process by planning the derivative pieces when you create the original.
