The core local SEO system applies to every service business. Each sector, however, has particular buyer concerns, rules and search behaviors that shape what works best.

## Lawyers and legal services

Buyers want reassurance, specialization and clarity. Create separate pages for each practice area, with plain-language explanations and realistic expectations. Show attorney bios, qualifications and bar memberships. Reviews matter, but follow professional conduct rules on advertising and testimonials in your jurisdiction. Make initial consultation booking easy and respect confidentiality in forms and case studies.

## Clinics and healthcare

Trust and accuracy are essential. Detail each treatment and condition with careful, accurate information, ideally reviewed by a qualified professional. Display doctor credentials, specialties and affiliations. Keep hours, appointment links and insurance information up to date. Health content is held to a higher standard, so cite reputable sources and avoid exaggerated claims. Check the advertising rules that apply to medical practices where you operate.

## Home services

Urgency and proximity drive these searches. Emphasize availability, response times and service areas. Use clear click-to-call buttons, service-area pages and photos of real jobs. Reviews with specific job details are especially persuasive. Service area business rules often apply.

## What they share

- A complete, accurate Google Business Profile with proper categories.
- Service pages that clearly explain each offering.
- A steady supply of recent reviews, answered promptly.
- Fast, mobile-friendly pages with a visible next step.
- Clear policies, credentials and contact details.

Start from the shared foundation, then adjust content, tone and compliance to the realities of your sector.
