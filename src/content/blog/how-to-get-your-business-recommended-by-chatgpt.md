When people ask ChatGPT for recommendations, such as 'who are good local SEO consultants' or 'how do I choose an accountant', the answer may name businesses or point to sources. You cannot buy your way into those answers, but you can improve the likelihood of being included.

## Understand how it gets information

ChatGPT can answer from its training data, and it can search the web when that feature is used, retrieving pages and citing them. Visibility therefore depends on being well represented in the broader web and on having pages that are accessible and useful for retrieval.

## Make your site accessible to its crawlers

OpenAI documents separate crawlers for different purposes, including GPTBot for training, OAI-SearchBot for search features and ChatGPT-User for user-initiated actions. Check your robots.txt and your hosting or security settings to make sure you are not blocking the ones you want to allow. Blocking is a legitimate choice, but if you want to be discoverable, allow access to the search-related crawler.

## Be clear about who you are and what you do

Create pages that state, in plain language, what you offer, who you serve, where you operate and what makes you credible. A model cannot recommend you for something your site does not clearly say.

## Build third-party corroboration

AI systems tend to favor claims that multiple independent sources support. Reviews on respected platforms, listings in industry directories, press mentions, guest articles, podcast appearances and community discussions all help. Aim for consistent descriptions of your specialty across these places.

## Publish genuinely useful content

Content that answers common questions in your field, includes specific detail and reflects real experience gives retrieval systems something worth citing. Original data, case studies and clear explanations are especially useful.

## Test, do not assume

Ask ChatGPT the questions your buyers would ask, in several phrasings, and note whether you appear, who does and what sources are cited. Repeat monthly. Results vary, so look for patterns over time, not a single answer.

## Avoid shortcuts

Do not try to manipulate responses with hidden text, fake reviews or mass-produced low-quality content. These tactics tend to backfire and can damage your reputation across all channels.
