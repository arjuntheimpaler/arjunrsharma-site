Prompts are the new queries, but they are longer, more conversational and more specific. People describe their situation in detail, then ask for a recommendation. Researching them helps you decide what content and presence to build.

## Where prompts come from

- Sales conversations: buyers often paste or paraphrase what they asked an AI tool.
- Client onboarding forms: add a question about how they researched options.
- Search Console: longer, question-like queries hint at prompt-style behavior.
- Community threads and forums, where people describe situations in detail.
- AI tools themselves: ask them for follow-up questions on a topic.

## Look for the pattern

Prompts usually include context: the business type, location, budget or goal. 'I run a small dental clinic in Pune and want more patients from Google, what should I do' is a typical example. Content that addresses that exact situation is easy to match.

## Turn prompts into assets

Group prompts by theme, then create pages and articles that address each situation in depth. Include the context terms such as industry, city and size. Use them in your testing set too, so you can track whether you start appearing.
