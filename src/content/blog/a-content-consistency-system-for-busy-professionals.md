Most personal brands fade not because of poor content, but because of irregular effort. A short, repeatable system protects consistency during busy periods.

## The weekly routine

- One hour for batch writing: draft three posts from your idea bank.
- Fifteen minutes to schedule them across the week.
- Ten minutes a day to reply to comments and engage with your audience.
- Thirty minutes once a week to capture ideas from client conversations.

## The monthly routine

Publish one longer piece, such as an article or case study, and review what worked. Update your idea bank and set your focus for the next month.

## Lower the barrier

Keep a notes app open for ideas. Use templates for common post types. Set a minimum standard, for example one post a week, that you can always meet. Missing a day should never mean quitting.

Protect the time in your calendar like a client meeting. Regular, modest output compounds into a visible reputation over a year.
