Answer Engine Optimization can feel abstract until it is broken into weekly tasks. This 90-day plan is designed for a service business with a modest team or a single marketer. Adjust the pace to your capacity, but keep the order.

## Days 1 to 30: Foundations and research

Week 1: Audit your site's technical basics. Confirm key pages are indexed, fast and mobile friendly. Fix anything blocking crawling.

Week 2: Collect questions. Pull them from sales calls, emails, Search Console and 'People also ask'. Aim for 50 to 100 real questions and group them by service and buying stage.

Week 3: Choose your priority pages, typically your top three to five service pages and your best-performing articles. Run the AEO content audit checklist on each.

Week 4: Review your entity information. Standardize your business name, description and contact details across your site, Google Business Profile and major directories. Add or fix Organization and LocalBusiness structured data.

## Days 31 to 60: Rewrite and build

- Rewrite the opening of each priority page with a clear definition and direct answers.
- Add or improve FAQ sections using your real questions.
- Restructure content with question-style headings, lists and tables.
- Publish four to six new articles answering high-value questions from your list.
- Add author bios and credentials, and link new content to relevant service pages.

## Days 61 to 90: Strengthen and measure

- Build trust signals: add case studies, reviews and sources.
- Earn a few relevant mentions and links through guest content, partnerships or local organizations.
- Set up your measurement dashboard with 15 to 20 target questions, tracking snippet ownership, positions, impressions, branded searches and leads.
- Review results, identify queries where you rank on page one but do not own the snippet, and refine those answers.

## After 90 days

Turn the process into a monthly routine: review questions, publish or update two to four pieces, check your dashboard and fix what is underperforming. AEO rewards steady maintenance more than one-off pushes.

Keep expectations realistic. Rankings and snippets can take time and change often. The practices in this plan, however, make your content clearer for customers and search systems alike, which is worthwhile regardless of how any single feature behaves.
