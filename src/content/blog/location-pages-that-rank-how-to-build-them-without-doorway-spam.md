Location pages are pages on your site dedicated to a specific place you serve, such as a branch, a city or a neighborhood. Done well, they capture local searches and give customers precise information. Done badly, they become thin, duplicated pages that search engines treat as low quality or as doorway pages.

## When to create one

Create a location page when you have a real presence or a real service offering in that place. A physical office or branch deserves its own page. A city you serve through a service area can justify a page if you can add genuine, useful local content. Do not create dozens of pages for towns you barely serve.

## What each page needs

- The exact name, address and phone number for the location, matching your profile and citations.
- Opening hours, directions, parking and transport information.
- A clear description of the services offered at or to that location.
- Staff, team members or local specialists, with photos.
- Local testimonials and reviews specific to that location.
- Case studies or examples of work done in the area.
- An embedded map and a clear call to action.
- Local details that only someone with real knowledge would include, such as neighborhoods, landmarks or regulations.

## Avoid the doorway trap

Copying one template across many pages and swapping only the place name creates near-duplicate content. Each page should offer information that would not make sense on any other page. If you cannot do that, consolidate into a single, strong service area page.

## Structure and links

Link location pages from a central 'locations' page and from the main navigation or footer. Link each to the relevant service pages, and link back. Use Local Business structured data on each, with its own address and details.

## Match your profile

If you have multiple profiles, link each profile to its own location page, not just your homepage. This reinforces relevance and helps customers land where they need.

Quality over quantity applies here. Five excellent location pages beat fifty thin ones.
