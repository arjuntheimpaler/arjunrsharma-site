You do not need an expensive platform to track GEO progress. A simple dashboard, updated monthly, shows whether your efforts are moving the numbers that matter.

## What to track

- Mention rate: the share of your test prompts in which your brand appears, by tool.
- Citation rate: the share in which your site is cited as a source.
- Sentiment and accuracy: whether the description is positive, neutral or negative, and whether it is correct.
- Competitor presence: which competitors appear most, and with which sources.
- Source mix: which third-party sites are cited most often for your topics.
- AI referral traffic: visits from AI tools in your analytics, where they send identifiable referrers.
- Leads and enquiries, including the answer to 'how did you hear about us' where AI tools are mentioned.

## Set it up

Create one sheet for your fixed list of 20 prompts and one for monthly summary metrics. Test each prompt in each tool on the same day each month, and log results the same way every time. In analytics, build a segment for referrals from known AI domains.

## Read it correctly

Because outputs vary, treat any single result with caution and watch the trend across several months. Pair the data with qualitative notes on what changed in your content and presence.

## Act on it

Each month, choose two or three gaps to address, such as a missing page, a weak source or an inaccuracy, and record what you did. The dashboard then becomes both a measurement tool and a log of what works.
