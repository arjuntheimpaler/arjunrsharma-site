Schema markup is code that labels the information on your pages in a standard format search engines understand. It does not directly raise rankings, but it helps search engines interpret your content accurately and can make you eligible for enhanced results.

## The types most useful to service businesses

- Organization or LocalBusiness: your name, logo, address, phone number, opening hours and social profiles.
- Service: what you offer, the area you serve and who it is for.
- FAQPage: question and answer pairs, where they appear on the page. Note that Google now limits when FAQ rich results appear, but the markup still clarifies content.
- Article: for blog posts, with author, date and headline.
- BreadcrumbList: the path to the current page.
- Review and AggregateRating: only where reviews are genuine and meet Google's guidelines.

## How to add it

Schema is usually added as JSON-LD, a block of structured data placed in the page. Many CMS plugins can generate it. Write markup that matches visible content on the page, since marking up things users cannot see violates guidelines.

## Test and monitor

Use Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator to check for errors, and review the Enhancements reports in Search Console. Keep details such as your phone number and address consistent with your Google Business Profile.

Start with your homepage and contact page using Organization or LocalBusiness markup, then add Article markup to posts and Service markup to key service pages. Accurate structured data is also a helpful foundation for AI-driven search, which relies on understanding who you are and what you do.
