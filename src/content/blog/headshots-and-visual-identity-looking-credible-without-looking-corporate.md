Visual identity does not require a design agency. It requires a few consistent decisions: the photo you use, the colors and fonts you apply and the style of your graphics. Consistency signals professionalism and makes you recognizable.

## The headshot

Invest in one good photo taken in natural light, with a clean background and a friendly, confident expression. Wear what you would wear to meet a client. Update it every couple of years so you look like the person they meet.

## Pick a simple palette and fonts

Choose one or two main colors and one neutral, plus one heading font and one body font. Use them on your website, slides, social graphics and documents.

## Use real photography

Photos of you working, speaking or with clients add authenticity and avoid the generic feel of stock images. Keep backgrounds simple and uncluttered.

## Keep it consistent

Use the same photo, name format and short description across LinkedIn, your website, speaker bios and directories. People should recognize you instantly wherever they meet you.
