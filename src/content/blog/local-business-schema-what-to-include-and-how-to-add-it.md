LocalBusiness schema is structured data that describes your business in a format machines can read. It reinforces the information on your Google Business Profile and website, and helps search engines connect them.

## Choose the right type

Schema.org offers specific subtypes of LocalBusiness, such as Dentist, LegalService, Plumber and ProfessionalService. Use the most specific type that fits.

## Include key properties

- name, url, logo and image.
- address, with street, city, region, postal code and country.
- telephone and email.
- openingHoursSpecification for each day.
- geo coordinates and areaServed for service areas.
- sameAs links to your official profiles on social and directory sites.
- priceRange, if appropriate.

## Rules

Keep the data identical to what users see on the page and on your profile. Do not mark up reviews you have written yourself, and follow Google's review markup guidelines. Add it in JSON-LD, and validate with Google's Rich Results Test.

For multi-location businesses, add separate markup on each location page with that location's details.
