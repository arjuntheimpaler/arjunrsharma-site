SEO, or search engine optimization, is the work of making your website easier for search engines to find, understand and trust, so that it appears when potential customers search for what you offer. For a service business, that usually means appearing when someone types something like 'accountant for freelancers' or 'commercial cleaning in Pune' at the moment they are ready to hire.

## How search engines decide what to show

Search engines run three broad processes. They crawl the web by following links, they index pages by storing and organizing what they find, and they rank results by deciding which indexed pages best answer a given query. SEO influences all three.

- Crawling: can the search engine reach your pages without being blocked or lost in a confusing structure?
- Indexing: does it understand what each page is about, and does it consider the page worth keeping?
- Ranking: does your page match what the searcher wants, and does your site look credible compared with competitors?

## The three pillars of SEO

Most SEO work falls into three buckets. Technical SEO covers the health of the site itself: speed, mobile usability, crawlability and clean URLs. On-page SEO covers the content on each page: the topic it targets, the headings, the copy and the internal links. Off-page SEO covers signals from outside your site, mainly links from other credible websites and mentions of your brand.

A weakness in any one pillar can cap results. A beautifully written page on a site that search engines cannot crawl will not rank. A technically perfect site with thin, generic content will not rank either.

## Why it suits service businesses

Service businesses sell high-value work to people who actively search for solutions. Someone searching for a solution already has the problem. SEO puts you in front of them without paying for every single click, and rankings compound over time because a page that ranks well keeps bringing visitors long after it is published.

## What a sensible first step looks like

Start with a short list of the services you most want to be hired for, and the questions your best clients asked before hiring you. Check that each service has its own page, that the pages load quickly on a phone, and that each page clearly says what you do, who it is for and how to contact you. That foundation matters more than any trick.

SEO is not a switch you flip. It is a system you build, measure and improve. The businesses that win are usually not the cleverest, they are the ones that stay consistent for long enough for the compounding to start.
