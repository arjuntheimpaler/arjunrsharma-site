For two years, my answer to “how do we submit our website to ChatGPT?” was the same: you can’t. That changed this week.

OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on November 10, 2026, and with it a dashboard called Business Hub. As someone who has spent six-plus years building growth channels, I think this is the most important AI search development of the year for marketers. It isn’t a ranking lever, though, and treating it like one will lead to disappointment. [searchenginejournal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/submit-website-chatgpt/596785/)

### What launched

Business Hub lets an authorized person claim a business, update its details, add a product feed with pricing and availability, and view aggregated insights. In plain terms, you can now tell ChatGPT the facts about your company and see how often it sends people your way. [dev](https://dev.to/daniwish/how-to-claim-a-business-in-chatgpt-and-the-data-that-should-match-first-3npn)

Two details stand out:

- **Online-only businesses qualify.** Unlike Google Business Profile, Business Hub accepts eligible online-only businesses, which opens the door for SaaS companies, publishers and D2C brands. [searchenginejournal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/submit-website-chatgpt/596785/)
- **Local coverage is still limited.** OpenAI’s setup guide says stores and restaurants can currently claim an existing U.S. listing, and early testers creating a new physical-location profile report hitting a waitlist. [searchenginejournal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/submit-website-chatgpt/596785/)

### How to claim your profile

The process takes under an hour if you have your paperwork ready.

1. **Find or create your profile.** Sign in to ChatGPT, open Business Hub from the sidebar and search for your business by name. [dev](https://dev.to/daniwish/how-to-claim-a-business-in-chatgpt-and-the-data-that-should-match-first-3npn)
2. **Verify your domain.** For a website business, this means adding a TXT record to your DNS, so bring in whoever manages your domain.
3. **Describe the business.** Choose a category and write a description. I’d write it in the language your customers use, not your internal positioning deck.
4. **Verify yourself and the company.** Expect an identity check with a government ID, plus your legal business name, address and registration details. Approved verification through Persona also adds a blue badge. [searchenginejournal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/submit-website-chatgpt/596785/)

The admin who claims the business can then invite teammates or grant an agency access. [searchenginejournal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/submit-website-chatgpt/596785/)

### What the data tells you

This is the part I care about most. Until now, AI visibility reporting relied on third-party estimates. Business Hub gives you first-party numbers: mentions, website clicks and discovery topics. [searchable](https://www.searchable.com/blog/chatgpt-business-hub)

Read them carefully:

- A mention only counts when ChatGPT references your website, so your brand name alone doesn’t register. [searchable](https://www.searchable.com/blog/chatgpt-business-hub)
- Insights cover the past 28 days and only appear above a threshold, so smaller brands may see little at first. [dev](https://dev.to/daniwish/how-to-claim-a-business-in-chatgpt-and-the-data-that-should-match-first-3npn)
- OpenAI says it does not share user conversations with the business. You won’t see the prompts people typed. [dev](https://dev.to/daniwish/how-to-claim-a-business-in-chatgpt-and-the-data-that-should-match-first-3npn)

My advice is to line these numbers up against your analytics for the same dates. Mentions without clicks suggest you’re being cited but not chosen. Clicks without conversions point to a landing page problem, not a visibility one.

### What it won’t do

I expect a wave of “claim your profile and rank higher” posts. Ignore them. OpenAI says claiming a business does not guarantee higher placement, and ChatGPT still makes its own recommendations and comparisons. [searchenginejournal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/submit-website-chatgpt/596785/)

Your profile also isn’t the only source ChatGPT uses. OpenAI’s profile policy says profiles can include information from other sources. If your pricing page, review sites and old press coverage contradict each other, a tidy profile won’t fix that. [searchenginejournal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/submit-website-chatgpt/596785/)

Multi-brand companies should plan for extra work too. There is no central way yet to manage a parent company’s brands and locations, and claiming the main brand does not extend to its locations. [searchenginejournal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/submit-website-chatgpt/596785/)

### My recommendation

Claim your profile this week. It’s free, it’s fast, and first-party data on a channel this size is worth having from day one.

Then do the unglamorous work around it:

- Make your business name, description and contact details consistent everywhere they appear online.
- If you sell products, look into the product feed so pricing and availability stay accurate.
- Keep earning mentions on the sites your buyers trust.

Business Hub is not a shortcut to visibility. It’s a measuring stick and a way to correct the record. The brands that pair it with strong content and a consistent reputation will pull ahead while everyone else waits for a ranking boost that isn’t coming.
