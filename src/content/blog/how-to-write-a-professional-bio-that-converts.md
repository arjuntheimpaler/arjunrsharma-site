A bio is read at the moment someone decides whether you are worth their time. Most bios list achievements in chronological order. The ones that work lead with the reader's problem and your relevance to it.

## A simple formula

Write one sentence on who you help and what outcome you create. Add one or two sentences of proof: experience, results or notable clients. Add a sentence of personality or a point of view that makes you memorable. Finish with a clear next step or location.

## An example

'Arjun R Sharma is a growth marketing consultant who helps service businesses build predictable lead flow through websites, SEO and AI search optimization. Over nine years he has worked with more than sixty businesses on strategy, content and technical growth. He believes marketing should be measured against revenue, not vanity metrics. He is based in Mumbai and works with clients worldwide.'

## Prepare three lengths

- One line, for social bios and event listings.
- Around 50 words, for guest posts and speaker pages.
- Around 150 words, for your website and press kits.

## Write in the third person for formal uses

Use the first person for LinkedIn and your own site, and the third person for speaker pages and publications.

Keep all versions consistent, since consistency across places builds recognition and gives search engines and AI tools a coherent description of who you are.
