Before buying any tracking software, run a manual audit. It teaches you how AI tools talk about your category and gives you a baseline to improve on.

## Step 1: Build a question list

Write 15 to 25 questions that your ideal buyers might ask an AI tool. Include several types.

- Category questions: 'what is the best way to choose an SEO consultant'.
- Recommendation questions: 'who are good local SEO specialists in Mumbai'.
- Comparison questions: 'SEO agency vs freelance consultant'.
- Problem questions: 'why is my website not getting leads'.
- Brand questions: 'who is Arjun R Sharma' or 'is [your business] good'.

## Step 2: Run them across tools

Ask each question in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode or AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude or Copilot. Use a fresh session without personalization when possible, and note whether web search was on.

## Step 3: Record the results

In a spreadsheet, log for each question and tool: whether you were mentioned, how you were described, which competitors appeared, which sources were cited and whether anything stated was wrong. Include the date.

## Step 4: Analyze

Look for patterns. Which questions never mention you? Which competitors keep appearing, and what sources cite them? Are the descriptions of you accurate, current and specific? Do the cited sources include sites where you could build presence?

## Step 5: Repeat

Run the same set monthly. Answers vary between runs, so ask important questions two or three times and focus on trends. Keep the list stable so comparisons are meaningful.

The result is a prioritized list of gaps: topics where you need content, sources where you need presence and inaccuracies you need to correct.
