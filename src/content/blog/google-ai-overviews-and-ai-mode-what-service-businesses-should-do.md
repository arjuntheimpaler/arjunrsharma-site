Google's AI Overviews appear above traditional results for many queries, summarizing an answer with links to supporting pages. AI Mode extends this into a more conversational search experience. Both draw on Google's search index.

## What Google says about eligibility

Google has stated that no special optimization is required to appear as a supporting link. A page needs to be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet. That means the basics matter: crawlable, indexable pages with good content.

## What this means in practice

Traditional SEO remains the foundation. If your pages are not indexed, are blocked by robots.txt or carry a noindex tag, they cannot be used. Beyond that, helpful, original, people-first content is what Google says it wants to surface.

## Practical priorities

- Check indexing and snippet controls. Make sure you are not unintentionally limiting snippets with restrictive directives.
- Answer questions clearly and completely, since these features synthesize across passages.
- Cover topics thoroughly, including related follow-up questions, because AI features often expand a query into multiple searches.
- Demonstrate experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust, with real author information and credible sourcing.
- Keep your Google Business Profile complete for local queries.
- Use structured data to clarify your business and content, keeping it consistent with visible text.

## Measure with care

Impressions and clicks from AI features are included in Search Console's overall performance data and are not reported separately. Watch overall trends and test key queries manually. Do not chase every change in the layout of results. Keep building a site that deserves to be cited.
