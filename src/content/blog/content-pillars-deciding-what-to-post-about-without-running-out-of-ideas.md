One of the most common reasons people stop posting is that they run out of ideas. Content pillars solve that. A pillar is a core theme you return to repeatedly. Choose a few, and you will always know what to write about.

## Why pillars help

They keep your content focused, so your audience learns what you are about. They reduce decision fatigue. And they build recognition, since repeating a handful of themes helps people connect your name with specific topics.

## How to choose them

Pick three to four themes at the intersection of what you know, what your clients care about and what you want to be known for. For a growth marketing consultant, these might be: practical SEO and AI search advice, case studies and results, how to run a consulting business and personal lessons from the work.

## Add formats

Under each pillar, vary the format. Use how-to posts, short stories, opinions, checklists, behind-the-scenes updates, client lessons and responses to questions. Mixing formats keeps the feed interesting.

## Build an idea bank

Keep a running list of ideas drawn from client questions, mistakes, wins and conversations. Aim to have twenty ideas waiting, so you are never starting from a blank page.

## Review and refine

Look at which topics earn replies, enquiries and conversations. Do more of what attracts the right people, not just what gets the most likes.
