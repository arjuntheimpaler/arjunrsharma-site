SEO and AEO are closely related, and treating them as rivals leads to confusion. A useful way to think about it is that SEO aims to get your pages ranked, while AEO aims to get your content chosen as the answer.

## The goal

SEO focuses on visibility in the list of results and on driving visits to your site. AEO focuses on being the source of a direct answer, whether or not the searcher clicks. Success in SEO is often measured in rankings and organic traffic. Success in AEO also includes snippet ownership, citations and brand mentions.

## The content style

SEO content can be long and comprehensive. AEO content needs the same depth, but organized so that specific answers can be lifted out cleanly. That means question-led headings, concise opening answers and clear structure.

## The technical layer

Both depend on a healthy site. AEO leans a little harder on structured data, clean headings and clear entity information, because machines must interpret the content accurately.

## Where they overlap

- Both reward helpful, original, trustworthy content.
- Both rely on crawlable, fast, well-structured pages.
- Both gain from authority signals such as links and brand mentions.
- Both depend on understanding search intent.

## Where they differ

- SEO optimizes mostly around keywords and pages. AEO optimizes around questions and passages.
- SEO measures clicks. AEO measures visibility inside answers, which may produce fewer clicks but more awareness.
- AEO puts more emphasis on brevity, directness and format.

## Do you need both?

Yes, and in practice they are the same workflow with an extra editing pass. Build the page with SEO in mind, then review each section and ask whether the answer to the main question is clear, direct and easy to quote. Doing both costs little extra and positions you for how people increasingly search.
