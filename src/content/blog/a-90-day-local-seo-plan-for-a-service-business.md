Local SEO is easiest to execute in phases. This plan suits a single-location or small multi-location business and assumes you are starting with an incomplete profile and a basic website.

## Month 1: Fix the foundation

- Week 1: Claim and verify your Google Business Profile. Collect your master business details.
- Week 2: Complete every profile section, choose categories carefully, add services, hours, attributes and photos.
- Week 3: Audit citations, then fix the main listings and correct duplicates.
- Week 4: Audit your website for consistency of name, address and phone, speed, mobile usability and click-to-call.

## Month 2: Build relevance

- Create or improve a dedicated page for each main service, with unique content and clear calls to action.
- Build location pages where justified, with real local information.
- Add LocalBusiness structured data and an embedded map.
- Launch your review request process, with a direct link and a simple script.
- Start responding to every review.

## Month 3: Build prominence

- Post regularly on your profile and add new photos.
- Reach out to local partners, associations and community organizations for listings and links.
- Pitch a local story or expert comment to local media.
- Publish one or two helpful local articles or guides.
- Set up tracking for calls, directions, form submissions and local rankings.

## Review at day 90

Compare your profile insights, review count, rankings and enquiries against your starting point. Identify the biggest remaining gaps, and plan the next quarter around them.

## Keep going

Local SEO is a maintenance discipline. Monthly tasks such as asking for reviews, answering questions, updating information and publishing useful content keep your visibility growing and protect it from competitors.
