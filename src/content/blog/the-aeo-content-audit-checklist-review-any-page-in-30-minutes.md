An AEO audit asks one question of a page: can a person or machine find, extract and trust the answer here? Use this checklist to review your most important pages. Score each item as pass, partial or fail, then fix the failures starting with your highest-value pages.

## 1. Question coverage

- Does the page target a clear primary question or topic?
- Does it address the related questions a buyer would ask next?
- Have you compared it against the questions in 'People also ask' for the topic?

## 2. Answer clarity

- Is there a direct answer in the first one or two sentences under each key heading?
- Are the answers self-contained, so they make sense if lifted out of context?
- Are definitions, costs, timelines and steps stated specifically?

## 3. Structure

- One H1, with logical H2 and H3 headings, ideally phrased as questions or clear topics.
- Lists for steps and options, and tables for comparisons.
- Short paragraphs and no walls of text.

## 4. Depth and usefulness

- Does the page go beyond what the top results already say?
- Does it include examples, data, templates or first-hand experience?
- Is it accurate and up to date, with a visible update date where relevant?

## 5. Trust signals

- Is the author named, with a short bio and credentials?
- Are claims supported by reliable sources where needed?
- Are reviews, case studies, certifications and contact details visible?
- Does the business information match your Google Business Profile and other listings?

## 6. Technical basics

- The page is indexable, fast and mobile friendly.
- The title tag and meta description are accurate and clear.
- Structured data is valid and matches visible content.
- Internal links connect it to related pages, and it is linked from relevant pages.

## 7. Conversion path

- Is the next step obvious? Does the page invite a call, a booking or a download?
- Would someone who found the page through an answer know why to choose you?

## Prioritize fixes

Fix in this order: indexability problems first, missing direct answers second, weak structure third and trust and depth after. Re-check the page in four to six weeks, and keep a record of what you changed so you can see what moves results.
