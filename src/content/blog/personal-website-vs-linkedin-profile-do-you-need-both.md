LinkedIn is an excellent place to be discovered and to build relationships. Your own website is the place where you control the message, the design and the data. Most consultants benefit from both, and they do different jobs.

## What LinkedIn does well

It offers a built-in audience, easy networking and quick proof of identity. It is ideal for building familiarity through regular posts and for people to verify your background.

## What your website does better

- You own it. Algorithms and policies do not control your access to your audience.
- You can present services, pricing guidance, case studies and detailed content in your own structure.
- It ranks in search engines and can be cited by AI tools, building long-term discoverability.
- It can convert visitors with forms, booking links and lead magnets.
- It gives you credibility, since many buyers expect a professional to have a site.

## How they work together

Use LinkedIn to attract attention and start conversations. Use your website to explain, prove and convert. Link between them. Keep your positioning, bio and visuals consistent across both.

If you must start with one, start with LinkedIn for speed, and build the website soon after, since it becomes your long-term asset.
