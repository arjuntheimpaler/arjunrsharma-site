GEO can seem overwhelming because it touches your site, your reputation and your measurement. Breaking it into three months of focused work makes it manageable. This plan assumes a small team and builds on solid SEO.

## Month 1: Baseline and foundations

- Run the manual AI visibility test across the main tools and record your baseline.
- Check that your key pages are indexed, fast and crawlable, and review robots.txt and security settings for accidental AI crawler blocking.
- Standardize your business description, name and contact information across your site, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and key directories.
- Add Organization or LocalBusiness and Person structured data, and strengthen your About page with credentials and clear specialization.
- Identify the 10 to 15 prompts that matter most for your services.

## Month 2: Content that can be cited

- Upgrade each core service page with a clear definition, process, timeline, cost guidance and FAQ.
- Publish a pricing or cost guide and at least one honest comparison page.
- Add or expand case studies with specific context and outcomes.
- Create two to four in-depth articles answering the prompts where you are absent.
- Add author bios, sources and update dates across your content.

## Month 3: Presence beyond your site

- Launch a steady review request process.
- Claim and complete profiles on relevant directories and associations.
- Pitch one or two guest articles or expert quotes to respected publications.
- Participate helpfully in two relevant communities, without promotion.
- Publish a small piece of original data if you can.

## Measure and refine

At the end of each month, rerun your prompt test and update the dashboard. At the end of month three, compare against your baseline: mention rate, citations, accuracy and leads. Choose the next quarter's priorities based on the gaps that remain.

## Expect a long game

Models update and retrieval changes, so results move gradually and unevenly. The reasonable aim is steady improvement in how clearly and widely you are described, supported by content and a reputation you would want anyway. That is the part you control.
