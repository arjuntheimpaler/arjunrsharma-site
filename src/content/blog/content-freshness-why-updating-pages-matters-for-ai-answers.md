Generative tools try to give accurate, current answers. Pages that clearly reflect recent information are easier to trust, particularly on topics that change, such as pricing, tools, regulations and platform features.

## What to update

Prioritize pages that attract traffic, support sales or cover fast-moving topics. Check facts, statistics, screenshots, tool names, prices and links.

## How to show freshness honestly

- Add a visible 'last updated' date when you have genuinely revised the page.
- Do not change dates without making real changes, since that undermines trust.
- Keep the URL the same when you update, so existing links and signals carry over.
- Remove or redirect content that is obsolete.

## A simple rhythm

Review your top 20 pages every six months and fast-moving pages every quarter. Keep a log of when each was reviewed and what changed. Updating an existing page is often more valuable than writing a new one.
