Your own channels grow slowly at first. Appearing on other people's platforms lets you borrow their audience and their credibility. A guest spot on a podcast or a short talk at an industry event puts you in front of people who already trust the host.

## Choose the right stages

Look for podcasts, newsletters, webinars and events whose audience matches your ideal client, not just those with the biggest numbers. A small, well-matched audience converts better than a large irrelevant one.

## Pitch with value

Hosts want to serve their audience. A good pitch is short and specific.

- Say who you are and why you are relevant, in a sentence or two.
- Offer two or three specific topics, with a clear benefit for listeners.
- Show you have done your homework, referencing a recent episode or event.
- Link to a short bio and something that shows how you communicate.

## Prepare well

Know your key messages, and prepare two or three stories and examples. Test your audio and video setup. Have a short, useful resource or link to offer listeners, so they can take a next step.

## Follow up

Thank the host, share the episode and tag them. Repurpose clips and quotes. Ask whether they know others who might want you as a guest. Add the appearance to your website and profile. Over time, a steady series of appearances builds name recognition and incoming links.
