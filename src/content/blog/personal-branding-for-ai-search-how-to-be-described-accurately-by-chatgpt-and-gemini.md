Buyers increasingly ask AI tools questions like 'who are the best SEO consultants for service businesses' or 'tell me about this consultant'. The answers shape first impressions. For an individual, the stakes are high, because a vague, wrong or absent description loses opportunities you never see.

## Test what AI says about you

Ask several AI tools about you by name, and about your category. Record whether you appear, how you are described, what is wrong or missing and which sources are cited. Include variations of your name and your business name.

## Why it may be inaccurate

AI tools draw on training data and web sources. If your information is sparse, outdated or inconsistent, or if another person shares your name, the tool may confuse details or say it has no information. The fix is to publish clear, consistent, corroborated information in places that machines can find.

## Build a clear identity

- Create a strong About page with your full name, role, specialty, location, experience, credentials and notable work.
- Add Person and Organization structured data, with sameAs links to your official profiles.
- Use the same short description across your site, LinkedIn, speaker bios, directories and guest articles.
- Use a consistent version of your name everywhere.

## Build corroboration

AI systems value claims that multiple credible sources support. Seek mentions in industry publications, podcasts, conference pages, directories and client case studies. Write guest articles with a proper author bio. Collect detailed recommendations on LinkedIn and reviews on relevant platforms.

## Publish expertise that can be quoted

Create clear definitions, frameworks, original data and in-depth guides under your name. Content with a distinct point of view and clear authorship is easier for tools to attribute to you.

## Allow access

Check that your robots.txt and security settings do not block search-related AI crawlers from your site if you want to be discoverable.

## Correct errors

If a tool states something wrong, strengthen the correct information at its source: update your pages, correct directory entries and add clear statements of the facts. Some tools offer feedback options. Results vary, so recheck periodically.

## Keep expectations realistic

You cannot control what any model says. You can make accurate information abundant, consistent and easy to find, which improves the odds across tools and also helps human buyers.

Treat AI visibility as a natural extension of personal branding: be clear about who you are, prove it publicly and keep it consistent.
