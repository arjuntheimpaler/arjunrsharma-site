Responding to reviews shows that you care and that a real person is running the business. Future customers read your replies as carefully as the reviews themselves, so each one is a small piece of marketing.

## Positive reviews

Thank the reviewer by name, mention something specific from their review and keep it short. Avoid copy-paste responses, since they feel automated. Naturally mentioning the service helps relevance.

## Negative reviews

- Respond promptly and stay calm. Never respond in anger.
- Acknowledge the problem and apologize where appropriate.
- Offer to resolve it offline, with a phone number or email, without sharing private details publicly.
- Do not argue, blame or reveal confidential information.
- If the review is fake, violates policy or does not relate to a real customer, report it, and consider a brief, factual public response.

## Mixed reviews

Thank the person for the feedback, acknowledge the positive part and address the concern directly.

## Make it routine

Set a time each week to reply, and aim to respond within a day or two. Consistent, thoughtful responses show reliability. If you can resolve an issue, ask the customer to update their review, but never pressure them.
