SEO and paid ads both put your business in front of people searching for your services, but they behave very differently. Choosing where to put your first marketing rupee or dollar depends on how fast you need leads and how much you can invest over time.

## Paid ads: fast but rented

Paid search can start producing enquiries within days. You choose your keywords, set a budget and control the message. The trade-off is that traffic stops when spending stops, and in competitive industries the cost per click can be high. Ads are excellent for testing demand, validating offers and filling the pipeline while other channels mature.

## SEO: slower but compounding

SEO usually takes months to build momentum, but pages that rank continue bringing visitors without a per-click charge. Over a long horizon, the cost per lead from SEO often falls well below paid channels, and organic results tend to earn more trust from searchers.

## How to decide

- Need leads within weeks and have a healthy budget: start with ads while you build SEO in parallel.
- Limited budget and a long-term view: lean toward SEO, particularly local SEO and service pages.
- Very competitive, expensive keywords: use SEO for supporting content and ads only on your highest-converting terms.
- Unsure what converts: run a small ad test to learn which messages and keywords produce real enquiries, then build SEO around the winners.

## The best answer is usually both

The two channels strengthen each other. Ads reveal which keywords convert, and SEO captures that demand cheaply over time. Your landing pages also improve for both. Start with the channel that fits your cash flow today, but plan for the mix you want in a year.
