Generative Engine Optimization, or GEO, is the practice of improving how often and how favorably your brand and content appear in answers produced by generative AI systems. Those systems include ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Microsoft Copilot.

## Why GEO is emerging

Many people now ask AI tools questions they once typed into a search box, including questions like 'which agency should I hire for local SEO' or 'what should I look for in an accountant'. The AI writes a synthesized answer, often naming a few options and citing sources. If you are not among the sources or names it uses, you are invisible at an important moment of the decision.

## How it differs from traditional search

In classic search, you compete for a position in a ranked list. In generative answers, there is no list to climb. The system retrieves material from several sources, or relies on what it learned in training, and composes a response. Your goal becomes being retrieved, trusted and mentioned.

## What influences visibility

- Whether your content is accessible to the AI's crawlers and search index.
- How clearly and specifically your content answers questions.
- How consistently your brand is described across the web.
- How often credible third parties mention and recommend you.
- How current and verifiable your information is.

## What GEO is not

GEO is not a set of secret tricks. Nobody controls these systems, and they change quickly. The practices that work tend to be the same fundamentals that build trust anywhere: clear information, real expertise, a good reputation and consistent presence.

## Where to start

Ask several AI tools the questions your customers ask, and see what they say about your category and about you. That quick test reveals where you stand and what to improve first. The rest of this series covers how to improve it step by step.
