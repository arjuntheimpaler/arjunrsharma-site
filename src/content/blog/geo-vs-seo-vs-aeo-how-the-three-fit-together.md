SEO, AEO and GEO are often presented as competing disciplines. In practice they are layers of the same work, each aimed at a different way people find answers.

## SEO: being found in search results

SEO focuses on traditional search engines and their ranked results. The aim is to rank pages and earn clicks.

## AEO: being the direct answer

AEO focuses on being selected as the answer in snippets, answer boxes and voice responses, by structuring content so answers are easy to extract.

## GEO: being included in generated answers

GEO focuses on AI systems that compose answers from multiple sources. The aim is to be cited and recommended within those responses.

## What they share

- Helpful, accurate, original content.
- Strong technical foundations.
- Authority and trust signals.
- A clear understanding of what the audience asks.

## What each adds

SEO adds keyword and page optimization and link building. AEO adds answer-first formatting and structured data. GEO adds attention to brand mentions across the web, third-party validation, crawler access for AI systems and monitoring of AI answers.

## One program, three lenses

Do not run three separate projects. Build strong pages that rank, format them so answers are easy to extract, and strengthen your presence beyond your own site so AI systems have reasons to name you. The same article can serve all three goals if it is well researched, clearly written and credibly attributed.
