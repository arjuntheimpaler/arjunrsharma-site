AI tools can already summarize common knowledge. What they cannot generate is information that does not exist elsewhere. Original data gives them something specific to cite, and gives people a reason to reference and link to you.

## What counts as original data

You do not need a large research budget. Useful sources include anonymized, aggregated results from your own client work, a survey of your customers or email list, analysis of public data that reveals a pattern others have not noticed, and benchmark ranges drawn from your experience.

## How to publish it well

- State the method, sample size and date, so readers can judge reliability.
- Present key findings in clear sentences and simple charts or tables.
- Avoid overclaiming. Report what the data shows and its limits.
- Update it regularly, for example annually, so it stays current.

## Respect privacy

Never expose client names or confidential details without permission. Aggregate and anonymize.

One solid piece of original research per year, promoted to relevant publications and communities, can earn mentions and citations long after publication.
