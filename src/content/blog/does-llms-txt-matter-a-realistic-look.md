llms.txt is a proposed convention in which a site places a markdown file at its root, such as yourdomain.com/llms.txt, listing its most important pages and providing short descriptions, intended to help language models understand the site.

## The current status

It is a community proposal, not an adopted web standard. As of this writing, major AI providers and search engines have not confirmed that they use it for ranking or retrieval, and Google has said it does not rely on it for search. Evidence that it improves AI visibility is limited.

## Is it worth creating?

It is cheap to produce and harmless, so some sites add one. Treat it as optional. A short, well-organized file that points to your core pages and describes what your business does costs little and may help tools that choose to read it, particularly for documentation-style sites.

## Where to spend effort first

Priorities with proven value include crawlable, indexable pages, clear content, accurate structured data, consistent business information and credible third-party mentions. If those are in place, adding llms.txt is a small extra. If they are not, it will not compensate.

Keep an eye on official guidance from the major platforms, since this area changes quickly, and avoid anyone selling llms.txt as a guaranteed route to AI citations.
