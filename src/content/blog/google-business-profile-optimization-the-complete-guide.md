Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a local customer sees, sometimes before they ever visit your website. A complete, accurate and active profile is the foundation of local SEO. This guide walks through each part in the order that matters most.

## 1. Claim and verify

Search for your business and claim the listing, or create one. Complete verification, which may involve a postcard, phone call, email or video, depending on your business. An unverified profile has limited visibility and control.

## 2. Use your real business name

Your name should match what appears on your signage, website and stationery. Do not add keywords or locations that are not part of your real name. Doing so violates Google's guidelines and can lead to suspension.

## 3. Choose categories carefully

The primary category is among the most important relevance signals. Choose the one that best describes your main business. Add secondary categories for other services you genuinely provide, but do not pad the list with loosely related ones.

## 4. Set your address and service area

If customers visit you, show your address. If you travel to customers and do not receive visitors, you can hide your address and define a service area. Do not use fake addresses or virtual offices.

## 5. Add accurate hours

Set regular hours, and add special hours for holidays. Incorrect hours frustrate customers and lead to poor reviews.

## 6. Write your description

Use the description to explain what you do, who you serve and what makes you different. Write naturally for people and avoid stuffing keywords or including promotional language or links that violate guidelines.

## 7. Add services and products

List each service with a short description. This improves relevance and helps customers understand what you offer. Add prices or price ranges if you can.

## 8. Upload photos and video

Add genuine photos of your exterior, interior, team, work and products. Profiles with fresh, real photos tend to attract more engagement. Avoid stock images. Add new photos regularly.

## 9. Use attributes and booking links

Choose attributes that apply, such as online appointments, wheelchair accessibility or languages spoken. Add appointment or booking links where available.

## 10. Post updates

Use posts to share offers, news and events. They keep your profile active and give customers current information.

## 11. Manage Q&A and messaging

Monitor questions asked by the public and answer them promptly. Seed the section yourself with common questions where allowed. Respond quickly if you enable messaging.

## 12. Collect and answer reviews

Reviews are a major prominence and conversion factor. Ask regularly, respond to all of them and handle negative feedback professionally.

## 13. Check insights and fix issues

Review performance data for searches, views, calls and direction requests. Watch for suggested edits from others and correct unauthorized changes.

## Maintain it

Treat your profile as a living asset. Check it monthly, update information promptly and respond to activity. A neglected profile loses ground to competitors who keep theirs fresh.
