Local SEO rewards basics done consistently. These are the mistakes I see most often, and each can be fixed without a large budget.

## 1. Unclaimed or unverified profile

Without control, you cannot update information or respond to reviews.

## 2. Keywords in the business name

Adding service words or locations to your name violates guidelines and risks suspension.

## 3. Wrong or too many categories

A vague primary category or irrelevant extras weaken relevance.

## 4. Inconsistent business information

Different phone numbers, old addresses and name variations across listings create confusion.

## 5. Ignoring reviews

Unanswered reviews and sparse recent feedback hurt trust and rankings.

## 6. Fake or incentivized reviews

These can be removed and can lead to penalties.

## 7. Thin, duplicated location pages

Swapping a city name across identical pages adds no value.

## 8. Poor mobile experience

Slow pages and hidden phone numbers lose customers who are ready to call.

## 9. No tracking

Without measuring calls, directions and enquiries, you cannot tell what works.

Audit yourself against this list. Fixing even a few of these can produce a noticeable lift.
