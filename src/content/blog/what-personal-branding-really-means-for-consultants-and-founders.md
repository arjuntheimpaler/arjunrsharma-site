Personal branding is the reputation you build for a specific kind of expertise, held in the minds of the people who might hire you. It is not a logo, a slogan or a filtered photo. It is what people say about you when you are not in the room, and what they expect from you before the first conversation.

## Why it matters for service businesses

Service buyers hire people, not products. Before a call, they usually look you up, read your profile, scan your site and ask around. A clear personal brand shortens that process: they already know what you do, who you help and why you are credible. That raises the quality of enquiries and reduces the effort of persuading.

## The three parts

- Positioning: what you are known for, and for whom.
- Proof: the evidence that you can deliver, such as results, case studies, credentials and testimonials.
- Presence: how consistently and visibly you show up, on your website, LinkedIn, other platforms and in your community.

## What it is not

It is not trying to be everywhere or sounding like everyone else. It is not inventing a persona. The strongest personal brands are honest, specific and consistent over a long time.

## A useful test

Ask three people who know your work to describe in one sentence what you do and who you do it for. If their answers differ widely from each other or from yours, your positioning needs sharpening. That is where to start.
