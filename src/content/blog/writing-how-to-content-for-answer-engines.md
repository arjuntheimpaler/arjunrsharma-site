How-to queries are everywhere, and they reward clarity. A well-written set of steps can be quoted in a snippet, read aloud by an assistant and followed by a reader without confusion.

## Start with the outcome

Open with one sentence stating what the reader will achieve and roughly how long it takes. Mention what they need before starting, such as tools or access.

## Write clear steps

- Use a numbered list, with one action per step.
- Begin each step with a verb.
- Include where to click or what to enter, and what the result should look like.
- Keep steps in the order they actually happen.

## Add the surrounding value

After the steps, add common mistakes, troubleshooting and tips. Add screenshots or short videos where they remove doubt. These extras are what make your page worth reading beyond the extracted answer.

Test your instructions by following them yourself, or ask someone unfamiliar with the task to try. Steps that fail in real use will quickly show up as lost trust.
