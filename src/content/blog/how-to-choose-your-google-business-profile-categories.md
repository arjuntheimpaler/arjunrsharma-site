Categories tell Google what your business is. They influence which searches you can appear for, so getting them right is one of the highest-impact choices on your profile.

## The primary category

Pick the category that best describes your core business, not the broadest or the trendiest. A business that mainly does family law should choose 'Family law attorney' over a general 'Law firm' if that option exists and fits. Specific, accurate categories usually serve better than generic ones.

## Secondary categories

Add other categories for services that you genuinely offer and that customers could reasonably search for. Do not add categories for services you do not provide, since that is misleading and can hurt relevance.

## How to research

- Search for your main service phrase and check the categories used by the top-ranking competitors. Several free browser extensions and tools can reveal them.
- Look at the category list Google offers as you type.
- Check that the categories match the services you list on your profile and website.

## Review regularly

Google adds, renames and removes categories over time. Revisit yours every few months, and test small changes one at a time so you can see their effect.
