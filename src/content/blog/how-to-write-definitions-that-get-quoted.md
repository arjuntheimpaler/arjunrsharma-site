'What is' searches are among the most common question types, and definitions are among the passages most often lifted into snippets and AI answers. Writing a strong definition is a skill worth practicing.

## The formula

Use a simple pattern: the term, what category it belongs to, and what distinguishes it. 'Technical SEO is the practice of optimizing a website's infrastructure so search engines can crawl, index and understand it.' One sentence, complete and clear.

## Then add context

Follow the definition with a second sentence on why it matters or how it is used, then expand into sections covering how it works, examples and common mistakes.

## Tips

- Place the definition directly under a heading that includes the term.
- Avoid starting with 'It is' or 'This is', since the passage should make sense alone.
- Avoid circular definitions that reuse the term.
- Be accurate. Wrong definitions lose trust quickly.
- Use the plain language of your audience before the technical terms.

Create a glossary page for your industry's key terms, linking each term to a deeper article. It builds topical authority and gives you many chances to be the quoted source.
