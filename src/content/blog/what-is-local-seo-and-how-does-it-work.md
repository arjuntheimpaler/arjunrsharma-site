Local SEO is the practice of improving your visibility in searches that have a location attached, either explicitly, like 'dentist in Andheri', or implicitly, like 'dentist near me'. It matters most to businesses that serve customers in a particular area, such as clinics, law firms, restaurants, trades and professional services.

## Where local results appear

Local searches often trigger the map pack, a block of three business listings shown with a map, as well as regular organic results and, increasingly, AI-generated summaries. Appearing in the map pack gives you prominent visibility, with calls, directions and website visits available directly from the listing.

## The three factors Google emphasizes

Google has said local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence.

- Relevance: how well your profile matches what the searcher is looking for.
- Distance: how far your business is from the searcher or the location named in the search.
- Prominence: how well known your business is, based on reviews, links, mentions and overall web presence.

## What you can influence

You cannot change distance, but you can influence relevance and prominence. Relevance comes from complete, accurate profile information, correct categories and service details, along with website content that matches the services you offer. Prominence comes from reviews, citations, links and a strong reputation.

## The main components

- Google Business Profile: the single most important asset for local visibility.
- Your website: service and location pages that match what people search.
- Reviews: quantity, quality, recency and your responses.
- Citations: consistent listings across directories and platforms.
- Local links and mentions: from community, press and partner sites.

## Where to begin

Claim and fully complete your Google Business Profile, make sure your name, address and phone number are consistent everywhere, and start collecting reviews. These steps alone often produce visible improvement for businesses that had neglected the basics.
