'Near me' searches express immediate local intent. The searcher wants results close to where they are right now. Search engines use the device's location, so you cannot target the phrase the way you target ordinary keywords.

## What actually drives the results

Results depend on the searcher's location, your profile's relevance and your prominence, as with all local searches. Writing 'near me' repeatedly on a page does not make you appear near the searcher.

## What to do instead

- Make sure your Google Business Profile is complete, accurate and verified, with the right categories.
- Include your full address and service area clearly on your website, using real place names.
- Build location pages with genuine local detail where appropriate.
- Keep reviews flowing and respond to them.
- Ensure your site is fast on mobile, since many near-me searches happen on phones.
- Make calling, directions and booking effortless with click-to-call buttons and clear hours.

## Understand the intent

Many of these searchers are ready to act, so the experience from search to contact must be smooth. A slow page, hidden phone number or outdated hours can lose the customer in seconds.
