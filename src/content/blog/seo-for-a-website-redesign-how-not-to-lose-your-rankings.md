A redesign is exciting for the business and risky for SEO. Rankings can drop when URLs change, content is trimmed, pages disappear or the new build is slower. Most of those losses are preventable with a plan that starts before design begins.

## Before the redesign

Benchmark your current performance. Record organic traffic, top landing pages, rankings for key phrases, conversions and the pages that attract links. Crawl the existing site and save the data. This tells you what to protect and gives you a baseline for comparison.

Identify your top-performing pages and make a rule that they keep their URL, their core content and their main headings unless there is a strong reason to change them.

## During the build

- Keep URL structures the same wherever possible. Where URLs must change, plan 301 redirects page by page.
- Preserve valuable content. Do not cut text to make a design look cleaner without checking what ranks.
- Keep title tags, headings, meta descriptions and internal links, or improve them deliberately.
- Make sure the new design is fast and mobile friendly, with proper heading structure and alt text.
- Build and test on a staging site, and block it from search engines while testing.

## Launch day checklist

Remove any noindex or robots blocking left from staging. Confirm redirects are working. Submit the new sitemap in Search Console. Check that analytics and conversion tracking are firing. Test forms, phone numbers and calls to action.

## After launch

Crawl the new site for broken links, redirect chains and missing pages. Compare traffic and rankings against your baseline weekly for at least two months. Expect some fluctuation as search engines reprocess the site, but investigate any sustained drop on key pages quickly.

## Involve SEO early

The biggest redesign mistakes come from decisions made without anyone thinking about search. Bring SEO into the project during planning, not after the design is signed off. A redesign handled this way can improve both the look of the site and its organic performance.
