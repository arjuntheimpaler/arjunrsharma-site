Traditional SEO reporting leans on clicks and traffic. AEO complicates that picture, because an answer can build awareness without sending a visitor to your site. Measuring it means widening the set of signals you watch.

## Core signals

- Snippet ownership: how many of your target queries show your page in a featured snippet or answer box. Rank trackers that report SERP features can show this, or you can check key queries manually.
- Impressions and click-through rate in Search Console, especially for question-style queries. Rising impressions with flat clicks may signal visibility inside answers.
- Position for question queries: track a set of target questions over time.
- Branded search volume: more people searching your name suggests growing awareness.
- Direct and referral traffic: increases can reflect people who saw your brand in an answer and came later.

## Business outcomes

Visibility is only useful if it contributes to growth. Track enquiries, calls and form submissions, and ask new leads how they found you. A simple 'How did you hear about us?' question on your form often reveals answer-driven discovery that analytics misses.

## Build a simple dashboard

Choose ten to twenty target questions. Record each month whether you own the snippet, your position, and the page that ranks. Add overall impressions, clicks and branded queries from Search Console, plus leads from the business side. Review it monthly and look for trends, not single data points.

## Be careful with attribution

No tool captures every answer appearance perfectly. Treat the numbers as directional, and focus on whether visibility and leads move together over a few months.
