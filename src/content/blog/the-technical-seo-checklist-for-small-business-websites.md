Technical SEO is the foundation under everything else. If search engines cannot crawl, understand or trust your site, great content will not help. This checklist walks through the essentials for a typical small business website, in a sensible order.

## 1. Crawlability

Make sure search engines can reach your important pages.

- Check your robots.txt file at yourdomain.com/robots.txt. Confirm it is not blocking important sections, and that a staging 'disallow all' rule did not get carried to the live site.
- Submit an XML sitemap in Google Search Console and confirm it lists only the pages you want indexed.
- Make sure important pages are reachable within a few clicks from the homepage through normal links.

## 2. Indexing

Check that the right pages are in the index and the wrong ones are not.

- Use the Pages report in Search Console to see which pages are indexed and why others are excluded.
- Look for accidental noindex tags on pages that should rank.
- Search 'site:yourdomain.com' for a rough sense of what is indexed and spot odd pages such as test or duplicate pages.
- Use canonical tags where several URLs show the same content, so one preferred version is clear.

## 3. Site structure and URLs

Keep URLs short, readable and consistent, such as /services/local-seo. Avoid long parameter strings and keep a logical hierarchy. Choose one version of your domain, with or without 'www', and one protocol, and redirect the others to it.

## 4. Redirects and errors

Fix broken links and pages that return 404 errors where they once held value. Use 301 redirects for permanently moved pages and avoid long redirect chains, which slow crawlers and users. Make sure deleted pages with backlinks point somewhere relevant.

## 5. Page speed and Core Web Vitals

Test key pages with PageSpeed Insights. Compress and properly size images, use modern formats, limit heavy scripts and plugins, and enable caching. Pay attention to the mobile results, since that is how most searchers will see you.

## 6. Mobile usability

Use a responsive design where text is readable without zooming, buttons are easy to tap, and content is not hidden on mobile. Search engines primarily evaluate the mobile version of your pages.

## 7. Security

Serve the whole site over HTTPS, make sure the certificate is valid and auto-renews, and fix mixed content warnings where secure pages load insecure resources.

## 8. On-page technical elements

- Each page has one clear H1 heading and a logical heading order below it.
- Unique title tags and meta descriptions on key pages.
- Descriptive alt text on meaningful images.
- Structured data where relevant, such as Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQ or Article.

## 9. Duplicate and thin content

Look for near-identical pages, such as location pages that only swap a city name, and either improve them with unique value or consolidate them. Thin pages with little useful content can dilute the quality of your site.

## 10. Monitoring

Set up Google Search Console and an analytics tool. Check coverage, performance and Core Web Vitals reports monthly, and review your site after any redesign, migration or plugin update, since these are the moments technical problems most often appear.

Work through the list in order, fix the items that block crawling and indexing first, and then tackle speed and structure. A clean technical base makes every other SEO investment work harder.
