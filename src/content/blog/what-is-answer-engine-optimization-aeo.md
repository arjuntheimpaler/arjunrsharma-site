Answer Engine Optimization, or AEO, is the practice of shaping your content so that it can be selected as the direct answer to a question. That answer might appear as a featured snippet at the top of Google, inside a 'People also ask' box, in a voice assistant's reply or within an AI-generated summary.

## Why AEO exists

Search has moved from a list of ten links toward direct answers. People increasingly ask full questions and expect a clear response without clicking through several pages. Search engines respond by extracting concise answers from pages that present information clearly. If your page is the source of that answer, you gain visibility, brand exposure and often a click from people who want more detail.

## What AEO looks like in practice

- Writing the answer to a question in a short, self-contained passage near the top of the section.
- Using clear question-style headings that match how people ask.
- Presenting steps, comparisons and definitions in lists and tables.
- Marking up content with structured data where appropriate.
- Building credibility so the answer is trusted.

## Who benefits

Service businesses benefit because buyers ask many questions before hiring: how much does it cost, how long does it take, what is included, is it worth it. Each question is an opportunity to be the clear, trustworthy answer, and being cited early builds familiarity before a prospect ever visits your site.

## How it relates to SEO

AEO is not a replacement for SEO. It sits on top of it. A page still needs to be crawlable, indexed and credible. AEO adds a layer of clarity: the page must also answer questions in a way that is easy to extract. The next articles in this series cover each part in practical detail.

## A first step

Pick one of your core services and list the ten most common questions clients ask before hiring. Answer each in a short paragraph on a page about that service. That single exercise captures the core of AEO.
