Generative tools compose answers from passages, so content that is specific, structured and self-contained tends to be easier to use. Academic work on generative engines has suggested that adding credible citations, direct quotations and relevant statistics can improve how often content is used, though results vary by topic and system.

## Formats that work well

- Clear definitions that state what something is in a sentence.
- Step-by-step guides with numbered steps.
- Comparison tables that line up options on the same criteria.
- Pricing guides with realistic ranges and what drives them.
- Checklists and templates.
- Original data, such as surveys or aggregated results from your own work.
- Case studies with named context, specifics and outcomes.
- Expert commentary with clear attribution.

## Qualities that help

Specific beats general. Verifiable beats vague. Attributed beats anonymous. When you include a statistic, say where it came from. When you make a recommendation, explain why.

Audit your top pages and identify one sentence on each that a person could quote on its own. If you cannot find one, the page needs sharper, more specific writing.
