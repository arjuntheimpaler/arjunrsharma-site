FAQ sections are a natural fit for AEO because they are built from questions and answers. But many FAQs are written badly, with vague questions nobody asks and marketing-heavy answers that say little. A strong FAQ earns its place by being genuinely useful.

## Use real questions

Draw questions from sales calls, emails, Search Console and 'People also ask'. Write them the way a customer would say them. 'How long does a website audit take?' beats 'Audit timeline information'.

## Keep answers direct

Start each answer with the answer. Aim for a short opening of two or three sentences, then add detail only if it helps. Include specifics such as ranges, timeframes and conditions instead of 'it depends' alone. If it truly depends, say what it depends on.

## Place FAQs where they help

A single generic FAQ page is less effective than FAQs on the pages where the questions arise. Put pricing questions on pricing and service pages, process questions on the process page and location questions on local pages. A short, relevant FAQ at the bottom of a service page often performs better than a site-wide one.

## Format properly

- Use real heading or accordion elements, with the question as a clear text element.
- Make sure the answers are visible in the page's HTML and not loaded only after interaction in a way crawlers cannot see.
- Mark up with FAQPage structured data if you wish. Google now shows FAQ rich results only for a limited set of sites, so do not expect a visual boost, but the markup still clarifies the content.

## Do not stuff

Ten excellent questions beat fifty weak ones. Remove questions that are only there to include a keyword. Review your FAQs every quarter, adding new real questions and updating answers that have gone stale.
