A zero-click search is one where the searcher gets what they need from the results page itself and never visits a website. Featured snippets, knowledge panels, maps and AI-generated summaries all contribute. A large share of searches now end this way, and the trend is likely to continue.

## Is that bad for you?

It depends on the query. For simple factual questions, such as a definition, a zero-click outcome offers little value. For commercial searches, such as 'best accountant for startups' or 'SEO consultant in Mumbai', the people deciding are still looking for providers to contact.

## How to adapt

- Focus on queries with commercial intent, where a click, call or direction request is the natural next step.
- Make your brand the one being named in answers, so recognition builds even without a visit.
- Optimize your Google Business Profile, since calls and direction requests can happen directly from results.
- Give people a reason to click: deeper detail, tools, templates, pricing guidance and examples that a snippet cannot contain.
- Track actions beyond sessions, including calls, bookings and branded searches.

## Accept the trade-off

Some visibility will not produce visits. That is fine if it builds familiarity with the people who later become clients. Measure the business outcome, not clicks alone.
