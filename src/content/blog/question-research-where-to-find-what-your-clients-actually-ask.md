AEO works best when you answer the questions your buyers actually ask, not the questions you assume they ask. Fortunately, those questions are easy to collect if you know where to look.

## Your own conversations

Start with sales calls, discovery meetings, emails, WhatsApp messages and support tickets. Note the exact wording. The questions asked right before someone hires you are the most valuable of all, because they reveal the doubts that need removing.

## Search features

- 'People also ask' boxes on results for your core topics.
- Autocomplete suggestions as you type a service phrase, particularly with prefixes like how, what, why, can and should.
- Related searches at the bottom of results pages.

## Your data

Search Console shows the questions people typed before landing on your site. Filter queries containing question words. On-site search data, if you have it, shows what visitors could not find.

## Communities

Forums, Reddit, Quora and LinkedIn groups show how people describe problems in their own words. Look for repeated themes and recurring confusion.

## Organize what you find

Group questions by stage: awareness, comparison and decision. Assign each cluster to a page, and answer each question in a short passage. Keep a running list and update it monthly. A steady supply of real questions makes consistent publishing much easier.
