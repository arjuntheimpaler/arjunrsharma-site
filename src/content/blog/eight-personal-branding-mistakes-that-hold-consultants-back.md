Skilled consultants often undersell themselves through avoidable habits. Here are the ones that matter most.

## 1. A vague position

'I help businesses grow' is not memorable. Be specific about who and how.

## 2. Inconsistent presence

Posting in bursts and then disappearing erodes momentum. Choose a pace you can sustain.

## 3. Talking only about yourself

Content centered on your achievements, not the reader's problems, gets ignored.

## 4. Copying others' voices

Imitating popular styles makes you generic. Write the way you speak.

## 5. Hiding your proof

Without case studies, results and testimonials, claims remain claims.

## 6. Spreading across too many platforms

Pick one or two where your clients are, and do them well.

## 7. Ignoring your own website

Relying on rented platforms leaves you without an asset you own.

## 8. Inconsistent information

Different bios, titles and photos across channels confuse people and search systems.

Choose one or two to fix this month and build from there.
