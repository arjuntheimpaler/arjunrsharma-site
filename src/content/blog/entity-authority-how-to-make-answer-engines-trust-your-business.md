Search engines and AI systems do not just read pages. They try to understand entities: the real-world people, businesses, places and concepts those pages describe. When your business is a clearly identified, well-corroborated entity, you are more likely to be trusted as a source of answers.

## What an entity is

An entity is something distinct and identifiable, such as a company, a person, a product or a location. Search engines build knowledge about entities from many sources and connect them to form a picture of who they are and what they are known for.

## Why it matters for AEO

When someone asks a question, systems prefer sources they can verify. If your name, expertise and services are described consistently across your site, your social profiles, directories and third-party mentions, systems can connect the dots and treat you as credible on the topic. If information conflicts or is missing, you are easier to overlook.

## How to strengthen your entity

- Publish a clear About page that states who you are, what you do, where you operate and your credentials.
- Use consistent naming, descriptions and contact details across every profile, directory and listing.
- Add Organization and Person structured data with links to your official profiles, using the sameAs property.
- Build and maintain profiles on relevant platforms such as LinkedIn, Google Business Profile and industry directories.
- Show real authorship on articles, with author pages that explain expertise and link to credentials.
- Earn mentions and links from credible sources in your field.

## Demonstrate experience

Content that reflects first-hand experience, such as case studies, specific examples and original data, helps establish expertise. Answers that are backed by visible experience are more likely to be considered reliable.

## Keep it consistent over time

Entity authority builds gradually. Audit how your business is described across the web twice a year, correct inconsistencies and update profiles. The effort pays off across SEO, AEO and AI-driven search at once.

Treat your online presence as a single, coherent identity. The clearer that identity is, the easier it is for any system to confidently use your content when answering a question.
