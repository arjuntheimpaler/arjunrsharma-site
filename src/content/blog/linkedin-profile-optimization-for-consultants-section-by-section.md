For many consultants, LinkedIn is the place where potential clients check credibility. A profile written like a résumé tells the visitor about your past. A profile written for buyers tells them how you can help. Here is how to rework each section.

## Profile photo

Use a clear, recent, well-lit headshot with a simple background, where your face fills most of the frame. Look approachable and professional for your industry. Avoid group photos, heavy filters and cropped holiday pictures.

## Banner

Use the banner to reinforce your positioning. State what you do and for whom, with a clean design and, if you like, a simple call to action or website address. Keep text away from the areas covered by your photo and buttons on mobile.

## Headline

The headline appears everywhere you do. Move beyond your job title. State who you help and how. For example: 'Growth marketing consultant | Helping service businesses get predictable leads from SEO and AI search.' Include the phrases buyers would search for.

## About section

Write this in the first person, in a natural voice. Open with the problem you solve, in language your client would use. Then explain how you help, share proof such as results or experience, and close with a clear next step. Keep paragraphs short. The first two or three lines are visible before 'see more', so make them count.

## Featured section

Pin your best assets: a case study, your booking link, a guide or a strong post. Choose things that move a visitor toward a conversation.

## Experience

For each role, describe the outcomes you delivered, not just responsibilities. Use specific results where possible and where confidentiality allows. Connect the story to your current positioning.

## Skills and recommendations

List relevant skills, and pin the most important at the top. Ask clients for recommendations that mention the problem, your approach and the result. A few detailed recommendations outweigh many generic ones.

## Contact information and custom URL

Set a clean custom profile URL, add your website and make sure your contact details are easy to find.

## Settings that help

Turn on creator mode features if useful, such as links and topics, and review your visibility settings. Consider enabling 'open to' services for providing consulting if suitable.

## Keep it current

Review the profile quarterly. Update it after new case studies, results or changes in focus. A profile that matches your website, bio and the way you speak builds a coherent identity.
