Local searches blend maps, reviews, snippets and AI summaries. Questions like 'which dentist near me offers weekend appointments' or 'how much does a lawyer cost in Delhi' are answered from a mix of sources. Being clear and consistent across those sources is the heart of local AEO.

## Start with your Google Business Profile

Local answers lean heavily on the profile. Complete every field, choose accurate primary and secondary categories, list your services, keep hours current, add real photos and respond to reviews. Use the Q&A and the description to state clearly what you do and where.

## Publish local, specific content

Create pages and articles that answer the questions people in your area ask, using local details. Cover pricing ranges, timelines, regulations, neighborhoods you serve and common local issues. Generic content is easily replaced. Local specifics are hard to copy.

## Keep your information consistent

Your name, address, phone number and service details should match across your website, profile and directories. Inconsistency makes it harder for systems to confirm who you are.

## Earn reviews that describe your services

Reviews that mention specific services and locations give answer systems rich material. Ask satisfied clients to describe what you did for them, in their own words, without scripting or incentives that violate platform rules.

## Add local structured data

Use LocalBusiness markup with your address, geographic area, opening hours and services. Make sure it matches your visible content.

Local AEO is mostly the same work as strong local SEO, with one extra habit: for every key service and neighborhood, ask what question a customer would ask, and make sure your site and profile answer it.
