Generative systems do not rely only on your website. They learn from, and retrieve from, a broad range of sources. How the rest of the web describes you strongly influences how an AI describes you.

## What co-citation means

When your brand is mentioned alongside certain topics, competitors or categories across multiple independent sources, those associations help systems understand what you are known for. If several credible sources describe you as a local SEO specialist for clinics, a model is more likely to connect you with that niche.

## Where mentions come from

- Industry publications and blogs, through guest articles and quotes.
- Podcasts, webinars and conference pages.
- Directories and associations in your field.
- Review platforms and community discussions.
- News coverage and local media.
- Partner and client websites.

## How to earn them

Offer genuine expertise: commentary, data and practical guides that publishers and communities find valuable. Participate in your professional community. Make it easy for people to describe you accurately by providing a clear short bio and consistent positioning.

## Keep the description consistent

Use the same core description of your business and specialty across every profile and mention you control. Consistency across sources makes it easier for systems to form a stable picture.

Think of mentions as reputation, not just links. They matter to AI systems and to human buyers alike.
