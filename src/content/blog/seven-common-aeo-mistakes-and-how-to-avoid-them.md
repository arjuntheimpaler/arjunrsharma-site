AEO is not complicated, but a handful of recurring mistakes undermine otherwise good content. Here are the ones I see most often.

## 1. Burying the answer

Long introductions before the point make answers hard to find. Put the answer first.

## 2. Vague, jargon-heavy language

Phrases like 'comprehensive solutions' say nothing extractable. Use specific, plain statements.

## 3. Ignoring real questions

Writing around keywords instead of the questions customers ask misses the point. Start from real questions.

## 4. No structure

Walls of text with no headings, lists or tables are hard to parse. Break content into clear sections.

## 5. Thin answers

A one-line answer with no depth offers no reason to click or trust you. Pair the direct answer with real substance.

## 6. Inconsistent business information

Conflicting names, addresses or descriptions across the web weaken trust. Standardize them.

## 7. Treating schema as a shortcut

Markup cannot make weak content authoritative. Fix the content first, then add structured data to clarify it.

Review your pages against this list, correct the mistakes you find and you will already be ahead of most competitors.
