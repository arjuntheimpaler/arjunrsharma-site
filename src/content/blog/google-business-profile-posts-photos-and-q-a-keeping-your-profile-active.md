A profile that was filled in once and forgotten looks stale. A little regular activity signals that the business is open and engaged, and gives customers fresh information.

## Posts

Use posts to share offers, updates, events and helpful tips. Keep them short, include a clear image and add a call to action button where available. Posting weekly or fortnightly is a good rhythm. Do not expect posts alone to move rankings dramatically, but they improve the customer experience.

## Photos

Upload real photos of your premises, team, work and products. Add new photos regularly, and encourage customers to share theirs. Check that the cover and profile images look professional. Avoid stock photos, which lack authenticity.

## Q&A

Monitor questions from the public, and answer promptly and accurately. You can add common questions and answers yourself, such as parking, pricing and appointment requirements. Check periodically for inaccurate answers from others and flag them.

Set a monthly reminder to review all three. It takes about twenty minutes and keeps your profile working for you.
